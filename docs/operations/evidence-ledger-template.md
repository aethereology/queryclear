Rules:

- Preserve the exact prompt-engine observation matrix before sampling: no more than 12 total rows distributed across no more than three named engines for the standard paid audit.
- Use one row per prompt × engine check. Every repeat is another observation and another row; never overwrite an unfavorable run with a favorable rerun.
- “Mentioned,” “cited,” “recommended,” and “ranked” are distinct observations.
- Mark unavailable/blocked/failed runs explicitly; the scheduled check still consumes one observation.
- Do not label an API/model response as ChatGPT, Claude, Google AI Overview, or another consumer product unless it is actually that named product and access mode.
- Do not present an inferred, cached, synthetic or modeled result in this live-observation table.
