# QueryClear legal and governance packet

Status: counsel, CPA, and management execution copies prepared August 25, 2026;
the Board consent remains a controlled draft until the executed exhibits and
complete voting-director roster are attached.

This directory separates four different records that should not be collapsed into one generic “approved” memo:

1. Management states the underlying facts and discloses exceptions.
2. Counsel selects and explains the legally approved operating structure.
3. The CPA selects and explains the tax and accounting treatment that matches that structure.
4. The Board authorizes the activity subject to the professional conditions and records conflicts, compensation controls, and continuing oversight.

## Execution order

1. Complete and sign `signature-packet/04-management-representations-and-implementation-certification.docx`.
2. Give counsel the management certification, governing documents, IRS exemption file, current contracts/policies, compensation and conflict records, budgets, and the draft Board consent.
3. Counsel revises and signs `signature-packet/01-counsel-legal-structure-concurrence.docx`.
4. Give the CPA the signed management and counsel documents plus the accounting records listed in the CPA memorandum.
5. The CPA revises and signs `signature-packet/02-cpa-tax-accounting-concurrence.docx`.
6. Attach the executed professional memoranda and sign `signature-packet/03-board-unanimous-written-consent.docx` with every director entitled to vote.
7. Place the complete packet in the corporate minute book and complete every initialed implementation control before scaled marketing.

An unsigned template is not an opinion, approval, tax position, Board action, or substitute for the professionals’ own engagement letters and workpapers.
The packet builder generates clean execution copies by default; run
`python tools/build-legal-signature-packet.py --draft` only when a fresh review
set is needed.
