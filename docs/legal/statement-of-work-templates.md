## Schedule A — $497 AI Search Audit

### Included scope

- Review of up to 10 public URLs selected for buyer and search importance.
- One crawl/technical review of accessible production pages.
- Seven-layer QueryClear readiness score with evidence and affected URLs.
- Up to 12 total prompt-engine observations distributed across up to three named answer engines selected at kickoff. One observation is one check of one exact prompt in one named engine; checking the same prompt in three engines consumes three observations, and any repeat check consumes another observation.
- Live results captured as dated point-in-time observations. Any modeled result is explicitly labeled and kept separate from live-engine observations.
- Prioritized findings with severity, rationale, recommended action, and estimated effort.
- One private report and one 45-minute remote walkthrough.
- One factual-correction round submitted within seven calendar days after delivery.

### Timing and fee

- Fee: $497 due at purchase.
- Delivery target: seven business days after complete intake and receipt of any agreed access.
- Upgrade credit: $497 may be applied once to an implementation SOW of at least $2,500 signed within 60 days after audit delivery. It is non-transferable and has no cash value.

### Excluded

Implementation, code changes, copywriting, design, competitor market research beyond context needed for findings, authenticated/private pages, exhaustive site crawling, continuous monitoring, regulated-claim approval, and guaranteed visibility outcomes.

### Acceptance

Accepted when the report includes the stated page limit, score/evidence, prompt-engine observation matrix, prioritized roadmap, and walkthrough invitation. Factual errors reported within the correction window will be corrected; differences in professional judgment or third-party engine output are not defects.

## Schedule D — AI Search Care Plan, $997 per month

This schedule is available only after QueryClear confirms that the foundational site is supportable. It is month-to-month, not “no contract.”

### Included each billing month

- One monthly seven-layer re-audit and concise change report.
- Up to two approved small updates, each requiring no more than 60 minutes of production work, with an aggregate maximum of two hours per month.
- One consolidated monthly planning/approval exchange by email or call, up to 30 minutes.
- Priority response target of two business days for ordinary requests.

Unused update capacity does not roll over. Larger work is quoted separately. Work begins only after required access, content, and approval are received.

### Excluded

Emergency/on-call response, redesigns, net-new sites, large page builds, migrations, custom development, security incident remediation, unlimited content, third-party costs, regulated-claim approval, and guaranteed search/AI outcomes.

### Billing and cancellation

$997 billed monthly in advance. Client may cancel through the provided customer portal or written notice before the next renewal; cancellation is effective at the end of the paid billing period. Unless required by law or caused by QueryClear’s uncured breach, partial months are not prorated.
