# Data Processing and Security Addendum — counsel-review draft

Version: 2026-08-25-draft. This addendum supplements the approved MSA when QueryClear processes personal data for a Client or receives access to Client systems.

## 1. Roles and scope

For personal data QueryClear processes solely to deliver services under an SOW, Client is the controller/business and QueryClear is the processor/service provider, unless applicable law assigns different roles. QueryClear may independently control limited account, billing, security, legal-compliance, and business-contact records as disclosed in its Privacy Policy.

Processing is limited to the duration, data categories, data subjects, systems, purposes, and instructions stated in the SOW and processing schedule. QueryClear will not sell or share Client Personal Data for cross-context behavioral advertising or use it outside the business purposes permitted by the agreement.

## 2. Client instructions and prohibited data

Client instructs QueryClear to process Client Personal Data only to perform the SOW, secure the service, comply with law, and follow documented lawful instructions.

Unless a separately signed regulated-data addendum expressly permits it, Client will not provide protected health information, medical records, payment-card data, government identifiers, biometric templates, passwords, children’s data, or other sensitive data not necessary for the services. QueryClear does not agree to act as a HIPAA business associate through this document.

## 3. Confidentiality and personnel

QueryClear will limit access to personnel and contractors who need it, ensure they are subject to confidentiality duties, provide appropriate security guidance, and remove access promptly when no longer needed.

## 4. Security measures

QueryClear will maintain measures appropriate to the services and risk, including:

- unique named accounts and multi-factor authentication where supported;
- least-privilege access, access review, and prompt offboarding;
- encryption in transit and provider-managed encryption at rest where supported;
- secrets stored outside source control and rotated after suspected exposure;
- staging, backup, rollback, and approval controls for production changes;
- signed webhook verification, idempotency, durable operational records, and abuse controls for public endpoints;
- authenticated or signed expiring client-report access and private/no-store caching;
- dependency, code, configuration, and vendor review proportionate to risk;
- documented incident response, recovery, and notification procedures; and
- secure deletion or return according to the retention schedule and legal obligations.

No security program eliminates all risk, and these measures do not constitute an absolute security guarantee.

## 5. Subprocessors

Client authorizes the subprocessors in QueryClear’s maintained subprocessor register. QueryClear will impose appropriate data-protection obligations on subprocessors and remain responsible for its contractual duties.

QueryClear will provide reasonable advance notice of a material new subprocessor when contractually required. Client may object on reasonable data-protection grounds within the stated notice period. The parties will work in good faith on a reasonable alternative; if none exists, either party may terminate the affected service without penalty for the unused period.

## 6. Requests, assessments, and cooperation

Taking into account the nature of processing, QueryClear will reasonably assist Client with data-subject requests, required risk assessments, regulator inquiries, breach obligations, and deletion/export requests. Additional work beyond the ordinary service may be billed at the SOW rate unless caused by QueryClear’s breach.

QueryClear will provide available summaries of relevant controls and vendor information. On reasonable notice and no more than annually, Client may submit a focused written security questionnaire. On-site audits require a demonstrated material concern, confidentiality protections, and reimbursement of reasonable costs.

## 7. Security incidents

QueryClear will notify Client without undue delay after confirming unauthorized access to, acquisition of, or material loss of Client Personal Data under QueryClear’s control. Notice will include available information about the nature, affected data, likely consequences, containment, and remediation. Notification is not an admission of fault.

Client is responsible for notifications concerning incidents in Client-controlled systems except to the extent caused by QueryClear.

## 8. Return, deletion, and retention

At the end of the services, QueryClear will return or delete Client Personal Data on written request, except for data retained in routine backups, minimal suppression/security records, legal holds, tax/accounting records, or records required to establish or defend legal claims. Retained data remains protected and is deleted when the exception ends.

## 9. International transfers and jurisdiction-specific terms

The parties will add any required transfer mechanism or jurisdiction-specific schedule before regulated cross-border processing. QueryClear does not represent through this draft that a particular transfer mechanism or privacy law applies.
