# Master Services Agreement — counsel-review draft

Version: 2026-08-25-draft. Do not use until approved by counsel.

This Master Services Agreement (“MSA”) is between **SparkCreatives Inc., a Florida not-for-profit corporation, doing business as QueryClear**, with an address at 6120 Caladesi Ct, Jacksonville, Florida 32258 (“QueryClear”), and the customer identified in an executed Statement of Work (“Client”). It becomes effective on the date of the first mutually executed Statement of Work.

Commercial service fees are payments for services and are not charitable contributions.

## 1. Structure of the agreement

Each Statement of Work (“SOW”) identifies the project, deliverables, assumptions, schedule, fees, acceptance criteria, and special terms. The MSA and all executed SOWs form the agreement. If they conflict, the SOW controls only when it explicitly identifies the MSA section it changes.

No proposal, email, purchase order, vendor portal, or Client form changes the agreement unless both parties expressly sign the change.

## 2. Services and changes

QueryClear will perform the services described in each SOW using commercially reasonable care. Work outside the stated page, template, prompt, revision, meeting, platform, integration, or time limits requires a written change order.

A change order must describe the added or removed work, price, schedule effect, and dependencies. QueryClear need not begin out-of-scope work before the change order is signed.

## 3. Client responsibilities

Client will:

- provide complete and accurate business, service, location, credential, price, review, claim, legal, and technical information;
- obtain all rights and approvals needed for content, trademarks, testimonials, images, software, accounts, and data supplied to QueryClear;
- appoint one decision-maker and provide consolidated feedback by stated deadlines;
- provide secure, least-privilege access through approved account-invitation methods, not passwords sent by email;
- maintain required licenses and approve regulated, medical, legal, financial, safety, or efficacy claims before publication;
- maintain backups unless the SOW expressly assigns backup responsibility to QueryClear; and
- avoid sending patient data, medical records, payment-card data, credentials, or other unnecessary sensitive data.

Delays in Client inputs, access, approvals, or third-party systems extend the schedule reasonably. After ten business days without a required Client response, QueryClear may pause the work. After thirty days, QueryClear may close the project and invoice completed work and non-cancellable commitments.

## 4. Fees, taxes, and payment

Fees and milestones are stated in the SOW. Unless the SOW says otherwise:

- invoices are due within seven calendar days;
- deposits and completed milestones are non-refundable except for an uncured material breach by QueryClear;
- Client is responsible for applicable sales, use, transaction, or similar taxes other than taxes on QueryClear’s net income;
- overdue undisputed amounts may accrue the lesser of 1.0% per month or the maximum allowed by law; and
- QueryClear may pause services after written notice while an undisputed invoice remains overdue.

Client must raise a good-faith invoice dispute within seven calendar days and pay the undisputed portion on time.

## 5. Review and acceptance

QueryClear will notify Client when a deliverable is ready for review. Client will either accept it or provide one consolidated written list of specific material nonconformities within five business days. The deliverable is accepted when Client approves it, publishes or uses it in production, or does not provide a timely rejection.

QueryClear will correct reproducible nonconformities with the written SOW at no additional charge. Preferences, new facts, changed direction, third-party changes, and work beyond included revisions are change-order work.

## 6. Search, AI, analytics, and third-party outcomes

QueryClear improves controllable website factors such as clarity, structure, crawlability, content, metadata, structured data, internal linking, local information, page experience, and conversion paths.

QueryClear does **not** guarantee rankings, traffic, leads, sales, inclusion in an index, rich results, AI citations, recommendations, or any specific statement by a search or AI system. Search engines, answer engines, browsers, hosting providers, models, datasets, and platform policies are operated by third parties and change without notice.

Any forecast, readiness score, modeled scenario, or “after” score is an analytical estimate, not a promised outcome. Live-engine observations are point-in-time samples affected by model version, account state, location, personalization, retrieval, and sampling variability.

## 7. Security, access, and data

Each party will use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the data and access it controls. QueryClear will use named accounts, multi-factor authentication where available, least privilege, and prompt offboarding. Client remains responsible for its security configuration except to the extent expressly assigned in an SOW.

The Data Processing and Security Addendum applies when QueryClear processes personal data on Client’s behalf. QueryClear may use approved service providers and subprocessors to deliver the work.

Neither party guarantees that any system is immune from interruption or unauthorized access. Each party will notify the other without undue delay after confirming a security incident materially affecting the other party’s data.

## 8. Confidentiality

“Confidential Information” means nonpublic information disclosed in connection with the agreement that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential. It excludes information the recipient can document was already known without restriction, independently developed, lawfully received from another source, or publicly available without breach.

The recipient will use Confidential Information only to perform or receive the services, protect it using reasonable care, and disclose it only to personnel and providers who need it and are bound by appropriate duties. A legally compelled disclosure is permitted after notice when legally allowed.

These duties last three years after disclosure; trade-secret duties continue while the information remains a trade secret under applicable law.

## 9. Intellectual property

Client retains its pre-existing materials, trademarks, accounts, data, and content (“Client Materials”). QueryClear retains its pre-existing and generally reusable methods, prompts, libraries, scoring systems, templates, components, tools, know-how, and improvements (“Background Materials”).

Upon full payment, QueryClear assigns to Client the custom final deliverables expressly identified as “Client-Owned Deliverables” in the SOW. QueryClear grants Client a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive license to any embedded Background Materials solely as necessary to use those deliverables.

Third-party software, fonts, stock assets, plugins, open-source components, platforms, and AI-tool outputs remain subject to their own terms. QueryClear will identify material third-party dependencies it selects. Client is responsible for continuing fees disclosed in the SOW.

QueryClear may not publish Client’s name, logo, testimonial, analytics, screenshots, or project details without a separate written release. General knowledge and de-identified learnings that do not disclose Client Confidential Information may be reused.

## 10. Warranties and correction period

Each party represents that it has authority to enter the agreement. QueryClear warrants that its services will materially conform to the applicable SOW and that it will not knowingly introduce malicious code.

For thirty days after production launch or delivery, QueryClear will correct reproducible defects in its implementation that cause the deliverable not to meet the SOW. The correction obligation does not cover Client or third-party changes, platform updates, unsupported environments, content inaccuracies, ordinary maintenance, or new requirements.

Except for the express warranties above, services and deliverables are provided “as is” to the maximum extent allowed by law.

## 11. Indemnification

Client will defend and indemnify QueryClear against third-party claims arising from Client Materials, Client-provided claims or instructions, Client’s unlawful use, or Client’s violation of another party’s rights.

QueryClear will defend and indemnify Client against a third-party claim that a custom Client-Owned Deliverable created solely by QueryClear infringes a United States copyright or trademark, excluding claims caused by Client Materials, Client instructions, combinations not supplied by QueryClear, modifications by others, or continued use after notice and a reasonable replacement is offered.

The indemnified party must promptly notify the indemnifying party, provide reasonable cooperation, and allow control of the defense, while retaining the right to participate at its own expense. No settlement may admit fault or impose non-monetary obligations on the indemnified party without consent.

## 12. Limitation of liability

To the maximum extent allowed by law, neither party is liable for indirect, incidental, special, exemplary, punitive, or consequential damages, or for lost profits, revenue, goodwill, business opportunity, or data, even if advised of the possibility.

Except for payment obligations, fraud, willful misconduct, confidentiality breaches, infringement/misappropriation, indemnification obligations, or liability that cannot legally be limited, each party’s aggregate liability arising from an SOW will not exceed the fees paid or payable under that SOW during the twelve months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

Counsel must confirm that these exclusions and caps are appropriate for the approved entity and insurance program.

## 13. Term and termination

The MSA continues while an SOW is active. Either party may terminate an SOW for a material breach not cured within ten business days after written notice. Either party may terminate immediately for unlawful conduct, insolvency, misuse of systems, or a material security threat.

On termination, Client will pay for accepted work, work performed through the termination date, and non-cancellable commitments. QueryClear will provide completed paid deliverables and reasonably assist with access handoff. Sections intended by their nature to survive will survive.

## 14. Independent contractors and personnel

The parties are independent contractors. The agreement does not create an employment, partnership, joint-venture, fiduciary, franchise, or agency relationship. QueryClear may use qualified employees and contractors and remains responsible for their performance under the agreement.

## 15. General terms

Notices must be sent to the contacts in the SOW, with legal notices to QueryClear at the address above and `hello@queryclear.com`. Neither party may assign the agreement without consent, except in connection with a merger, reorganization, or sale of substantially all relevant assets, provided the successor assumes the agreement.

Neither party is responsible for delay caused by events beyond reasonable control, except payment obligations. The agreement is the complete agreement on its subject and may be amended only in a signed writing. Waiver of one breach is not waiver of another. Invalid provisions will be narrowed or severed while the rest remains effective. Electronic signatures and counterparts are permitted.
